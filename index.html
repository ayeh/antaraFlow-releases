Every chat.
One window.

antaraFlow puts WhatsApp, Telegram, Mail and your other messaging services side by side in one Mac app. Each keeps its own login, and your unread count sits in the Dock.

Download for Mac

Needs macOS 13 or later.
Notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.

What you get

Separate logins
Every service runs in its own page with its own sign-in, so accounts never mix.
Work and personal, apart
Switch between workspaces to show only the services you need right now.
One inbox you can search
Recent messages from every service in one list, with search across all of them.
Unread counts you can see
A badge in the Dock and a count in the menu bar, even when the window is closed.
Locks with Touch ID
antaraFlow locks when your Mac's screen locks, and opens with Touch ID or your password.
Private by default
Settings stay on your Mac. AI apps such as Claude can read your messages only if you turn that on, read-only and on this Mac.

Install

From the download

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg.
  2. Drag antaraFlow into Applications.
  3. Open it from Applications. After that it updates itself.

With Homebrew

Newer Homebrew asks you to trust a tap before it uses it. The second line does that for this one only.

brew tap ayeh/antaraflow
brew trust ayeh/antaraflow
brew install --cask antaraflow
